Privacy policy

Last updated October 5, 2026

This policy explains what information Manoj Mortgages collects through this website, why, and what you can ask of us. In short: we only collect what you choose to send, we use it to help with your mortgage, and we never sell it.

Who we are

Manoj Mortgages is the business of Manoj Patel, a licensed Mortgage Agent Level 2 (FSRA licence licence # to come) with brokerage to come (FSRA brokerage licence number to come). Manoj Patel is responsible for the information collected on this website.

What we collect

  • What you send through the survey: your situation, what the mortgage is for and when, rough ranges for the property value, down payment, credit and income type, and your name, phone number, email address, best time to call and any message.
  • What you tell us by phone, text or email when you contact us directly.
  • Basic website statistics, only if analytics is switched on: pages visited and how people arrived, collected by Google Analytics using cookies. It does not include what you type into the survey.

The survey never runs a credit check. Nothing is collected unless you choose to send it.

Why we use it

  • To contact you about your enquiry, by phone, text or email
  • To understand your situation and tell you which mortgage options may fit
  • If you decide to go ahead, to prepare and submit your mortgage application
  • To keep the records that Ontario mortgage brokering rules require

Your consent

When you send the survey, you agree that Manoj Patel can contact you about your enquiry. We won't add you to marketing emails or newsletters without asking separately. You can withdraw your consent at any time by calling or emailing us; we'll stop contacting you, except where we must keep records by law.

Who we share it with

  • Lenders and mortgage insurers, only when you ask us to apply for a mortgage on your behalf, and only what the application needs.
  • Our brokerage, brokerage to come, which oversees our work and keeps records as Ontario rules require.
  • Service providers that run this website, the survey form and email for us. They may store information outside Canada, where it can be subject to the laws of that country, and they may only use it to provide their service to us.

We never sell your information or share it for anyone else's marketing.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information only as long as we need it to help you, or as long as mortgage brokering rules require us to keep records of a mortgage we arranged. After that, it is deleted or securely destroyed.

How we protect it

The website is served over a secure, encrypted connection, and access to your information is limited to Manoj Patel and the service providers above. No method of sending or storing information is perfectly secure, so please don't send account numbers, your social insurance number or similar details through the survey.

Your rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where the law allows. Contact us at manojmortgages@gmail.com or 647-824-8251. If you're not satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada.

Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your information, we'll update this page and the date at the top.
