Mortgages for newcomers to Canada

Moved to Canada recently? You can buy a home before you've built years of Canadian credit. There are programs built for exactly this.

Start the 2-minute surveyCall 647-824-8251

How newcomer mortgages work

Several lenders and mortgage insurers have programs for people who arrived in Canada in the last few years. They accept a shorter Canadian credit history, and some accept other proof that you pay your bills on time, such as rent or utility payments.

What you'll usually need

  • Your permanent resident card or work permit
  • A job letter and recent pay stubs from your Canadian employer
  • Proof of your down payment, including any funds from outside Canada
  • Whatever Canadian credit history you have so far

Down payment

Permanent residents can often qualify with as little as 5% down through insured newcomer programs. Work-permit holders usually need more. I'll tell you which program fits your status on the first call.
