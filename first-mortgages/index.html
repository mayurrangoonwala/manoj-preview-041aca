Mortgages for buying a home

A first mortgage is the main loan on the home you buy. I shop it across lenders, get you pre-approved, and stay on the file until you have the keys.

Start the 2-minute surveyCall 647-824-8251

How it works

  • A short call or the survey, so I understand your income, savings and credit
  • A pre-approval that tells you your budget and holds a rate while you shop
  • Once you have an accepted offer, I take the file to the lender and handle the conditions
  • Your lawyer closes, and I check in before your first renewal

Down payment

In Canada the minimum is 5% of the first $500,000 and 10% of the portion above that, on homes up to $1.5 million. Above $1.5 million, or with less than 20% down on an uninsured lender, it's 20%. With under 20% down the mortgage needs default insurance, which is added to the loan.

Qualifying

Lenders test whether you could afford the payment at a higher rate than the one you get, often called the stress test. If that rules you out at a bank, a B-lender may still approve you on different terms.
