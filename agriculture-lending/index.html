Agriculture and farm lending

Farms and rural property need lenders who understand them. Many banks treat acreage as unusual, but there are lenders who specialise in it.

Start the 2-minute surveyCall 647-824-8251

What I help with

  • Buying a working farm or adding land
  • Hobby farms and rural homes on acreage
  • Refinancing farm property to fund equipment, buildings or a family transfer

How it differs

Lenders usually value the house and a limited parcel of land under residential rules, and look at a working farm as a business. Down payments are often higher, and a farm lender may want your farm income and business plan.

Questions about agriculture lending
