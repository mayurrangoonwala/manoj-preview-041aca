Mortgage renewal

When your term ends, your lender sends a renewal offer, and many people simply sign it. It's the one time you can move without a penalty, so it's worth a second look.

Start the 2-minute surveyCall 647-824-8251

Why shop your renewal

Renewal offers are not always the best rate the lender has. Comparing before you sign can save real money over the next term, and switching at maturity has no prepayment penalty.

When to start

Start about four months before your term ends. Many lenders will hold a rate for up to 120 days, so you can lock in early and still take a lower rate if one appears.

If things have changed

If your income has dropped, you are now self-employed, or your credit has taken a hit, a straight renewal may be all your bank offers. I can check whether a better option exists, including with B-lenders.
